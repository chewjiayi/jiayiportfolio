SIT x Glasgow Hackathon

AI Innovation

The SIT x Glasgow Hackathon challenges participants to leverage AI creatively and effectively to deliver real business impact. Teams are required to design AI-powered solutions that address practical industry problems, focusing on innovation, usability, and measurable value to users or organisations.

AI Figma Business Impact

For the SIT x Glasgow Hackathon, my team developed an AI-powered recruitment screening tool designed to help recruiters evaluate candidates fairly and efficiently. The system automatically extracts key information from uploaded resumes—such as skills, years of experience, and education level—and generates an objective ranking based on predefined criteria. By relying on structured data instead of human judgment, the solution reduces bias, speeds up shortlisting, and supports recruiters in making more consistent and data-driven hiring decisions.

OCBC Ignite Challenge

Pitch · Business Model

The OCBC Ignite Challenge is an innovation competition where participants select a theme from real OCBC problem statements—such as ATM Services and Beyond—and develop creative solutions supported by a functional prototype. The challenge focuses on applying design thinking, strategy, and product innovation to solve modern banking and customer experience problems.

Pitching Innovation

Our group selected the theme ATM Services and Beyond, focusing on designing a customizable ATM interface that adapts to the needs of three key user groups: elderly users, adults, and children. The goal was to create a more inclusive, intuitive, and user-friendly banking experience for all ages.

Blue Ocean Innovation Competition

Social Impact

The Blue Ocean Competition challenges participants to create innovative solutions using Blue Ocean Strategy frameworks. Teams identify unmet customer needs, redesign value propositions, and propose strategic moves that open new market space.

Business Strategy Innovation

I designed MindMesh, a concept that helps people capture ideas the moment they happen. Using a small EEG ear-clip, it detects creative brainwaves and prompts users to save their thoughts, which AI then organizes into clean notes.
